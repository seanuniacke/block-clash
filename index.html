📖 How to Play

🖱️ Desktop Controls:

Click and drag a piece to the board, OR click any piece in the tray to select it and click any cell on the board to place it.

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls:

Touch and drag pieces directly onto the board with your finger, or tap a piece in the tray followed by tapping a board cell.

🎯 Win Conditions:

Survival Trap: Points do not decide the game. The winner is the player who outlasts their opponent when the other player cannot place any piece remaining in their tray.

Points Race: The first player to reach or exceed the target score (1,000 to 10,000+ points) wins immediately.

🗿 Obstacles (Bedrock):

Levels 2 to 6 feature indestructible stone obstacles that cannot be cleared by line clears, creating strategic chokepoints.
