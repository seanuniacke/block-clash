How to Play:

• Take turns dropping shapes onto the shared 8×8 board.

• Completely filling any horizontal row or vertical column clears it for +100 pts.

• Win Condition: Trap your rival! If they have no valid spots to place any of their remaining 3 pieces on their turn, you win!
