📖 How to Play

🖱️ Controls:

Drag a piece directly onto the board, OR tap/click a piece in the tray to select it and tap/click any cell on the board to place it.

🏟️ 4-Player & 2v2 Teams (10x10 Board):

4P Free-For-All: 4 players battle on an expanded 10x10 board. Trapped players are eliminated until one survivor remains (or hits the Points Race goal).

2v2 Teams: Players 1 & 3 (Team Cyan) face Players 2 & 4 (Team Rose). Teammates share colors, icons, and combined team scores! If one teammate is trapped, their partner can clear lines to free up space for them.

🎯 Win Conditions:

Survival Trap: Outlast your opponents when they cannot place any piece in their tray.

Points Race: First player or team to reach the target score wins immediately.
